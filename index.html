MouseConfig

Configure your Redragon M913 gaming mouse — DPI, buttons, RGB — right from your browser on Windows, Mac, or Linux. No vendor software, no drivers to install.

Not connected. Works in Chrome or Edge — on macOS, grant Input Monitoring access when prompted.

Profile

Have a .jmk file (Redragon's Windows profile export) shared by someone on a forum or Discord? There's no way to open a .jmk file on Mac or Linux with Redragon's own software — import it here instead to bring in its button mappings. DPI, polling rate, and LED aren't decoded yet, so those stay at defaults.
